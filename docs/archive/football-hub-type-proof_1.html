What to look at

The control has almost no responsive type — only the h1 changes below 640px. The candidate re-sizes six roles. That is the structural difference; the rest is voice.

Where they diverge most

Body goes 14 → 17 and metadata leaves uppercase Sora for lowercase mono. Those two moves, not the h1, are what change how the page feels at reading distance.
