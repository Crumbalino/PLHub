## 4. Leeds, Coventry, Hull missing from homepage and nav

**Cause (verified live): slug mismatch, not missing config.** The 7 Aug inventory/#24 diagnosis is out of date.
- `clubs` table (in_scope, 20 rows): `leeds-united`, `coventry-city`, `hull-city`. `posts.club_slug` carries them too (39 / 15 / 4 rows).
- `src/config/clubs.ts` and `src/lib/clubs.ts` (since #71) key them `leeds`, `coventry`, `hull`.
- `getInScopeClubs()` (`src/lib/stats.ts`, ~L104-160) selects DB slugs and calls `getClub(row.slug)` → `undefined` for those three → dropped, logged only via `console.error`. Result: **17** links on `/` (counted from served HTML), and `FactsBlock` says "Clubs covered 17" beside a 20-club league.
- The pages themselves are fine: `/leeds`, `/coventry`, `/hull` return 200 and are in the sitemap. `/leeds-united` returns 404. They are just unlinked from the nav, so the sitemap is their only route in.

**Smallest fix (one file, no migration):** in `getInScopeClubs()` build the list from `CLUBS` in `@/lib/clubs` — the registry that already drives routes, `generateStaticParams` and the sitemap — and delete the Supabase query and the `@/config/clubs` import. ~10 lines; the nav then equals the sitemap by construction.
**Not covered, flag for #24:** `posts.club_slug` uses DB slugs while `src/lib/club-matcher.ts` imports `src/config/clubs.ts` (`leeds`). Whether new Leeds posts are written as `leeds` and rejected by the FK was **not verified**. Check before re-plugging any club feed.
